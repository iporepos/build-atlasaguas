Harmonized and Enhanced Geospatial Dataset from the 2021 Brazilian Water Atlas by ANA

This dataset provides a harmonized and enhanced geospatial version of the 2021 Brazilian Water Atlas (Atlas Águas) by Brazil's National Water Agency (ANA).

 

Harmonization

The original Atlas layers and spreadsheets are combined into a single relational GeoPackage (EPSG:4326). Spatial layers hold only a key and the geometry; their attributes live in linked tables with primary and foreign keys. Municipalities, intakes, aqueducts, supply systems, indicators, investments and proposals are all included. Two catalog tables, tables and fields, document every table and column in Portuguese and English, with units.

Enhancement: intake catchments

The dataset adds the upstream catchment (contributing drainage area) of every surface-water intake point: 4,063 polygons, which are not available in the original Atlas. Each catchment was traced upstream through HydroBASINS (levels 8 and 12) and refined with the HydroSHEDS 3 arc-second DEM, with manual corrections where needed. The catchments_attributes table records the delineation method, the geodesic area (km²) and the number of polygon parts of each catchment. Catchments join to intakes and supply systems through intake_code. Catchments found to be wrong are fixed in later versions; the method value then ends in manual_replaced or _manual_edits.

Files

  • T2_ANA_ATLASAGUAS_DB_BRAZIL_2021.zip: the database, ANA_ATLASAGUAS_DB_BRAZIL_2021.gpkg. This is the file most users need.
  • T1_ANA_ATLASAGUAS_DB_BRAZIL_2021.zip: the standardized source files the database is built from, one file per Atlas layer (CSV or GeoPackage) with a glossary CSV each (column, Portuguese and English description, unit), plus the layer catalog ANA_ATLAS-AGUAS_LAYERS_X_X.csv. Provided for reproducibility.

Getting Started

Unzip T2_ANA_ATLASAGUAS_DB_BRAZIL_2021.zip and open ANA_ATLASAGUAS_DB_BRAZIL_2021.gpkg in QGIS or any GeoPackage-aware tool (19 data tables, 2 catalog tables and 2 views).

Start with the metadata. The tables catalog lists every table with its kind, row count and description; the fields catalog describes every column (type, key, unit, Portuguese and English description). Use them as the guide to everything else.

  • Spatial layers (key + geometry): municipios, municipios_sedes (urban seats), intakes, aqueducts, catchments and provisioning_areas.
  • Attribute tables, joined by key: municipios_attributes (municipio_code), intakes_attributes and catchments_attributes (intake_code), aqueducts_attributes (aqueduct_id), provisioning_areas_attributes (municipio_code).
  • Indicators and plans: municipios_indicators (urban population and water demand for 2020 and 2035; water security index for 2020), municipios_investments, proposals and proposals_municipios.
  • Supply systems: systems, systems_supply (system × municipality, with share) and intakes_systems (intake × system); the view systems_supply_population derives the population served.
  • Provisioning areas: provisioning_areas is, for each municipality, the union of the catchments of the intakes that supply it (linked through intakes_municipios and the supply systems, listed in the view provisioning_links); provisioning_areas_attributes gives the water source mix, intake counts, area and topology.

Data Source

Agência Nacional de Águas e Saneamento Básico (ANA). Atlas Águas: segurança hídrica do abastecimento urbano. 2nd edition. Brasília: ANA, 2021. http://atlas.ana.gov.br. Metadata: ANA Metadata Catalog.

Original data access is free provided the source is cited.
