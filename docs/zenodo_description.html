Harmonized and Enhanced Geospatial Dataset from the 2021 Brazilian Water Atlas by ANA

This dataset provides a harmonized and enhanced geospatial version of the 2021 Brazilian Water Atlas (Atlas Águas) by Brazil's National Water Agency (ANA).

 

Harmonization

The original Atlas layers and spreadsheets are combined into a single relational GeoPackage (EPSG:4326). Spatial layers carry their own attributes (no join needed in QGIS); plain tables are linked to them by primary and foreign keys. Municipalities, intakes, aqueducts, supply systems, indicators, investments and proposals are all included. Two catalog tables, tables and fields, document every table and column in Portuguese and English, with units.

Enhancement: intake catchments

The dataset adds the upstream catchment (contributing drainage area) of every surface-water intake point: 4,063 polygons, which are not available in the original Atlas. Each catchment was traced upstream through HydroBASINS (levels 8 and 12) and refined with the HydroSHEDS 3 arc-second DEM, with manual corrections where needed. The catchments layer records the delineation method, the geodesic area (km²) and the number of polygon parts of each catchment. Catchments join to intakes and supply systems through intake_code. Catchments found to be wrong are fixed in later versions; the method value then ends in manual_replaced or _manual_edits.

Files

  • T2_ANA_ATLASAGUAS_DB_BRAZIL_2021.zip: the database, ANA_ATLASAGUAS_DB_BRAZIL_2021.gpkg. This is the file most users need.
  • T1_ANA_ATLASAGUAS_DB_BRAZIL_2021.zip: the standardized source files the database is built from, one file per Atlas layer (CSV or GeoPackage) with a glossary CSV each (column, Portuguese and English description, unit), plus the layer catalog ANA_ATLAS-AGUAS_LAYERS_X_X.csv. Provided for reproducibility.
  • ANA_ATLASAGUAS_SYNTHESIS_MUNICIPIOS_BRAZIL_2021.csv: a flat table derived from the database, one row per municipality (5,572), for use without GIS software. It brings together the municipality attributes, the 2020 and 2035 indicators, the supply shares (surface and groundwater) and covered population, the provisioning area attributes (total and inside the municipality), the investments and the number of proposals. Semicolon-separated, UTF-8; text values in double quotes; an empty cell means no value.
  • ANA_ATLASAGUAS_SYNTHESIS_MUNICIPIOS_BRAZIL_2021_GLOSSARY.csv: description of every column of the synthesis table, in Portuguese and English, with units.

Getting Started

Unzip T2_ANA_ATLASAGUAS_DB_BRAZIL_2021.zip and open ANA_ATLASAGUAS_DB_BRAZIL_2021.gpkg in QGIS or any GeoPackage-aware tool (16 data tables, 2 catalog tables and 2 views).

Start with the metadata. The tables catalog lists every table with its kind, row count and description; the fields catalog describes every column (type, key, unit, Portuguese and English description). Use them as the guide to everything else.

  • Spatial layers, each with its attributes: municipios (municipio_code), municipios_sedes (urban seats), intakes and catchments (intake_code), catchments_transfers (transfer_id), aqueducts (aqueduct_id), provisioning_areas and provisioning_areas_within (municipio_code).
  • Indicators and plans: municipios_indicators (urban population and water demand for 2020 and 2035; water security index for 2020), municipios_investments, proposals and proposals_municipios.
  • Supply systems: systems, systems_supply (system × municipality, with share) and intakes_systems (intake × system); the view systems_supply_population derives the population served.
  • Provisioning areas: provisioning_areas is, for each municipality, the union of the catchments of the intakes that supply it (linked through intakes_municipios and the supply systems, listed in the view provisioning_links), with the water source mix, intake counts, area and topology, and the surface intakes of other municipalities inside it (upstream) with the population their systems serve; gaps between neighbouring catchments are filled. Where a river receives water from another river through a pipe or canal, the source area is added too: catchments_transfers holds, per receiving (target) intake, a copy of the catchment of the source intake (with or without the intake itself being part of the transfer, see transfer_type) or, where the source has no Atlas intake, a drawn polygon; n_intakes_transfer and n_transfer_areas count the source intakes and the drawn polygons per municipality. provisioning_areas_within is the part of each provisioning area inside the municipality's own boundary (parts under 0.01 km² dropped), with its area, its share of the provisioning area, the percent of the municipality it covers and its number of parts; its geometry is empty when the municipality is supplied entirely from outside.

Data Source

Agência Nacional de Águas e Saneamento Básico (ANA). Atlas Águas: segurança hídrica do abastecimento urbano. 2nd edition. Brasília: ANA, 2021. http://atlas.ana.gov.br. Metadata: ANA Metadata Catalog.

Original data access is free provided the source is cited.
